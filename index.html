Every number and curve on this page is read from the cached evaluation (bundles, W-cache, records.jsonl) — no model runs. Heatmap and token shading are the selected method's attribution, normalised per family (image / text). RISE = AUC of the deletion curve; MAS adds the mass-alignment penalty |MR−DR|. γ is recomputed live from the cached edge matrix; metrics snap to the nearest evaluated γ ∈ {0.5, 1}. Δ on a sample card is min-RISE(all baseline arms) − min-RISE(ours), image leg: positive = ours beats every baseline on that sample.
